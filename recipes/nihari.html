Nihari

Description

Nihari is a traditional South Asian slow-cooked stew made with tender beef or mutton and a rich blend of aromatic spices. The dish originated during the Mughal era and has become one of the most beloved meals in Pakistan and Northern India.

Nihari is typically cooked overnight on low heat, allowing the meat to become incredibly soft and flavorful. It is often garnished with fresh ginger, green chilies, coriander, and lemon before being served with naan.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef or mutton
  • 2 onions
  • Ginger garlic paste
  • Nihari masala
  • Oil or ghee
  • Water

Steps

  1. Heat oil and fry onions.
  2. Add ginger garlic paste.
  3. Add meat and cook for 10 minutes.
  4. Add Nihari masala and water.
  5. Cook on low heat until tender.
  6. Serve with naan.
