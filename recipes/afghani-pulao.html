Afghani Pulao

Description

Afghani Pulao, also known as Kabuli Pulao, is Afghanistan's national dish. It combines fragrant basmati rice with tender mutton, sweet carrots, raisins, and carefully selected spices.

The combination of savory meat and sweet toppings creates a unique flavor that has made this dish popular throughout Central and South Asia. It is commonly served at weddings, family gatherings, and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g mutton
  • 2 carrots
  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 1 onion
  • Cumin seeds
  • Cardamom pods
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Cook the mutton until tender.
  2. Wash and soak rice.
  3. Cook carrots and raisins separately.
  4. Add rice to meat stock.
  5. Top with carrots and raisins.
  6. Steam until rice is fully cooked.
