Mutton Karahi

Description

Mutton Karahi is a famous Pakistani and North Indian dish prepared in a traditional cooking vessel called a karahi. It features tender mutton cooked with tomatoes, green chilies, ginger, and a blend of aromatic spices.

Unlike many curries, Mutton Karahi focuses on the natural flavors of the meat and tomatoes. It is best enjoyed with naan, roti, or freshly baked tandoori bread.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg mutton
  • 6 large tomatoes
  • 6 green chilies
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • Fresh ginger strips
  • 1/2 cup cooking oil
  • Red chili powder
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a karahi.
  2. Add mutton and cook until browned.
  3. Add tomatoes and spices.
  4. Cook until tomatoes soften.
  5. Add green chilies and ginger.
  6. Cook until meat is tender and serve hot.
